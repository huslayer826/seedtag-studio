# Outreach

SeedTag Studio should be shared as a free operations helper for garden clubs, seed libraries, and plant sale volunteers. Avoid region-specific plant advice unless a local organizer supplies it.

## Short Pitch

SeedTag Studio turns one crop list into printable plant tags, seed packet labels, care cards, table signs, and a frost-date task calendar. It runs as a static browser app, uses local import/export, and does not need paid services.

## Email Opener

Subject: Free label and calendar kit for your spring plant sale

Hi [Name],

I built SeedTag Studio for garden clubs and seed libraries that need cleaner labels without setting up accounts or paid tools. You can add crops, print tags and packet labels, export a CSV backup, and download an ICS task calendar from your last frost date.

Demo: https://huslayer826.github.io/seedtag-studio/

Would this help your volunteers prep for the next swap or plant sale?
