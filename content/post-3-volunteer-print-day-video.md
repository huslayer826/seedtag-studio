# Video Draft: Volunteer Print Day in 60 Seconds

## Hook

"Your plant sale labels do not need to be rebuilt from scratch every spring."

## Shots

1. Open SeedTag Studio with the sample event.
2. Change last frost date and sale date.
3. Add tomato, basil, zinnia, and coneflower from starter data.
4. Edit one donation amount and care link.
5. Switch from plant tags to seed packet labels.
6. Download the ICS calendar.
7. Print the table sign.

## Close
