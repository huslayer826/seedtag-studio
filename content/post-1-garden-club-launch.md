# Post Draft: A Cleaner Plant Sale Label Day

Garden club plant sales often run on donated starts, handwritten notes, and a few volunteers trying to keep everything organized. SeedTag Studio gives the team one shared label batch for plant tags, seed packet labels, care cards, and table signs.

Start with built-in crops, adjust the donation field, add your local care link, and print. The same batch can export CSV for the next volunteer and ICS calendar tasks from your last frost date.

Demo: https://huslayer826.github.io/seedtag-studio/
